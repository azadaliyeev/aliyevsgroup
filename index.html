Privacy policy

When people comment on or send messages to the company's own Instagram and Facebook accounts, the company's software processes the comment or message text, the sender's public username and the platform identifiers that Meta provides, only to reply to that comment or message.

  • Data is stored on the company's own server and is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Data is kept only as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of replies, then deleted.
  • To have your data deleted, email the address above. Requests are completed within 30 days.
